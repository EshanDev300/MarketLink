1. Executive Summary & Problem Definition

1.1 Background & Necessity

Local farmers markets are growing rapidly across urban and suburban communities as health-conscious shoppers seek fresh, seasonal, and locally grown agricultural produce. Despite rising popularity, traditional market operations suffer from acute communication bottlenecks and logistical friction:

  • Shopper Uncertainty & Wasted Trips: Consumers rarely know in advance which farmers will attend a particular market day, what varieties they will bring, or at what prices. Popular seasonal items (such as organic berries or sourdough) frequently sell out within the first 30 minutes, resulting in wasted commutes and frustration.
  • Farmer Harvest Miscalculation & Waste: Growers lack pre-harvest demand visibility. Over-harvesting causes unsold perishable surplus at the end of hot market days, causing agricultural food waste. Under-harvesting deprives farmers of potential revenue.
  • Lack of Spatial Coordination: Stalls are informally distributed, making it challenging for customers with tight schedules to locate vendors and pick up orders quickly.
💡 The MarketLink Vision: MarketLink transforms local farmers markets by establishing a digital pre-order loop. Farmers publish weekly recurring inventory templates prior to harvest day, and customers reserve produce baskets with specific morning pickup windows. Pre-orders are paid for in-person at pickup, protecting growers from credit card gateway deductions and ensuring fair trade.

1.3 Out-of-Scope Clarifications (As per SRS Page 8)

In strict compliance with the TechWiz 7 SRS guidelines:

  • No Online Payment Gateways: Payment for pre-orders is settled 100% in person at market pickup (Cash, Card, or Mobile Pay at the stall).
  • No Courier / Delivery Logistics: The platform explicitly supports market-day pickup only, eliminating delivery carbon emissions.
  • No Food Safety / Certification Auditing: Government licensing verification is handled by physical market organizers outside the application scope.

3. Functional Requirements & Role Breakdown

3.1 Customer Features

  • Registration & Authentication: Full profile with name, email, phone, and address. Role-based login with JWT security.
  • Browse Markets & Interactive Map: Filter markets by day (Saturday, Sunday, Wednesday) and city. View stall locations on OpenStreetMap with direct navigation links.
  • Produce Catalog & Multi-Criteria Filtering: Filter by Category (Vegetables, Fruits, Dairy, Bakery, Honey, Herbs), price range, market, and in-stock availability.
  • Pre-Order for Market Pickup: Add produce to harvest basket, choose pickup date and morning time window (e.g. 09:00 AM – 10:00 AM). Stock is automatically reserved.
  • Order Management & Modification: Track status timeline (Placed → Accepted → Ready for Pickup → Completed). Modify pickup time slot or cancel before farmer cutoff time.
  • 1-Click Re-Order: Instant re-order feature for recurring weekly staples.
  • Favorites & Restock Alerts: Bookmark favorite farmers and staple produce for quick weekly access.
  • Ratings & Reviews: Post 1–5 star reviews with comments after order completion.

3.2 Farmer / Vendor Features

  • Stall Profile Management: Set stall name, contact person, market location, operating days, pickup time windows, and cutoff hours.
  • Weekly Stock & Pricing Management: Full CRUD operations for products (name, category, price, unit, stock quantity, harvest day, photo). Support for recurring weekly templates.
  • Toggle Sold-Out Status: 1-click toggle to mark items sold out or restore availability as harvest updates.
  • Pre-Order Fulfillment Workflow: View incoming customer pre-orders, accept or decline with cancellation reasons, mark orders "Ready for Pickup", and record in-person completion.
  • Farmer Sales Insights: Real-time KPI summaries: Total Revenue, Pending Orders, Active Listings, and Best-Selling items.
  • Customer Review Responder: View customer reviews and post public grower responses.

3.3 Administrator Features

  • Executive KPI Dashboard: Platform metrics: Total Farmers, Pending Approvals, Total Customers, Active Markets, Total Pre-Orders, and Revenue across all hubs.
  • Farmer Verification & Approval: View new farmer registrations and approve or suspend them before they can list products.
  • Customer Account Moderation: Activate or suspend customer accounts in case of policy violations.
  • Farmers Markets CRUD: Add, edit, or remove community markets with GPS coordinates, operating schedules, and directions.
  • Content Moderation: Inspect and remove inappropriate product listings or customer reviews.
  • Global Announcements: Broadcast platform-wide alerts and market news to farmers and customers.

3.4 Built-in AI Market Assistant (24/7)

An intelligent, domain-trained chatbot embedded in every page. It queries live database records to answer customer questions about:

  • Operating hours and days for each farmers market.
  • Farmer attendance, stall numbers, and pickup time windows.
  • Product availability (e.g., "Find organic heirloom tomatoes").
  • How pre-orders work and payment settlement at the stall.

  • Node.js: v18.0.0 or higher (Tested on Node.js v24.18.0)

  • Browser: Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
