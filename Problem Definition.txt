1. BACKGROUND AND CONTEXT
Local farmers markets are experiencing tremendous growth as conscious consumers
seek fresh, seasonal, nutrient-dense, and locally grown produce. However, under
traditional market dynamics, both farmers and customers encounter persistent friction:

a) Shopper Uncertainty & Wasted Trips:
   Shoppers rarely know in advance which farmers will attend a particular market
   day, what crop varieties will be available, at what prices, or whether specific
   produce will sell out minutes after gates open. Availability is typically
   communicated informally through chalkboards, printed flyers, or social media
   posts that lack real-time synchronization. Shoppers arrive to find high-demand
   staples sold out or discover that a grower is closed for the week.

b) Vendor Stock Management & Harvest Planning:
   Growers lack a streamlined channel to broadcast weekly harvest availability,
   forecast demand prior to harvesting, and secure pre-orders. Over-harvesting leads
   to post-market food waste and financial loss, while under-harvesting results in
   unmet customer demand.

c) Geographic & Pickup Coordination:
   Traditional markets lack integrated digital mapping and schedule clarity,
   making stall identification and pickup logistics inconvenient for working
   families and time-conscious buyers.

2. PROPOSED SOLUTION: MARKETLINK (eGREEN BASKET)
MarketLink is a responsive full-stack Web application that bridges local growers
and consumers on a unified, transparent platform:

• Digital Stalls & Weekly Inventory:
  Farmers establish verified stall profiles, configure recurring weekly stock
  templates, adjust quantities based on upcoming Friday harvests, and specify
  pickup hours.

• Predictable Pre-Orders with In-Person Settlement:
  Customers browse real-time inventory, filter by category/market/day, reserve
  baskets, and select convenient morning pickup slots. Pre-orders are paid for
  in person directly at the stall (Cash / Card / Mobile Pay), preserving fair trade
  and eliminating third-party payment gateway fees.

• Embedded OpenStreetMap & Geolocation:
  Interactive maps display market coordinates, stall locations, and route directions
  for seamless order pickup.

• Role-Based Governance:
  Dedicated dashboards tailored for Customers, Farmers, and System Administrators.

• Built-in AI Intelligent Assistant:
  Assists customers 24/7 with inquiries regarding market hours, vendor attendance,
  produce availability, and pre-order pickup procedures.

3. GOALS AND ANTICIPATED IMPACT
- 100% elimination of wasted trips through pre-order reservation guarantees.
- Significant reduction in farm-level harvest waste through predictable pre-demand.
- Direct economic empowerment of regional smallholder agriculture.
- Convenient, transparent, and sustainable community food ecosystem.
